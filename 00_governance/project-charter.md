# Project Charter

## Purpose

Neo UO exists to create a distinct survival-war sandbox using Ultima Online as the chassis.

The project is not being built primarily to chase scale, nostalgia capture, or direct competition with the largest existing shards. It is being built to realize a specific world experience: a place where life has weight, movement matters, conflict escalates into pursuit, and survival can be more meaningful than simple kill counts.

## Product Intent

Neo UO should offer:

- meaningful death and survival tension
- a warfare-first open world
- drawn-out, readable conflict
- slower, more consequential progression
- stronger political and territorial stakes over time
- a detached arena lane that shares the same combat grammar

## Audience Intent

Neo UO is aimed at:

- veteran UO players looking for a different kind of shard
- survival-minded and hardcore sandbox players
- players who enjoy hunting, evasion, risk, and world consequence
- new players who may enter through the arena or through the distinct survival identity

## Project Standard

Every major feature should be judged against this question:

Does this make Neo UO feel more like a dangerous lived-in survival-war world, or does it pull the game back toward ordinary shard behavior?
