# Phase64D1F - Movement and Waypoint Observability

## Status

Active next.

## Objective

Add observability for movement and waypoint state without enabling autonomous movement execution.

## Starting Baseline

- Phase64D1E Selective Sleeve Descent Preview runtime is accepted.
- Preview receipts can map future movement-related intents without dispatch.
- Tactical dispatch remains disabled.
- Movement ownership and movement leases remain governed by existing services.

## Required Boundaries

- No autonomous movement activation.
- No waypoint editing unless separately authorized.
- No route visualization unless separately authorized.
- No ClassicUO modification.
- No gameplay adapter invocation.

## Queue Afterward

- Sparse Waypoint Editor and Recovery Network.
