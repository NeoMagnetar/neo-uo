# Decision Log

## 2026-04-15 — portable arena rules over hardcoded graveyard logic
The project chose a portable arena rules framework over hardcoded Britain graveyard logic.

### Reason
The Britain graveyard wall area is the first test region, but the long-term system needs to expand into larger arena region instances without forcing a rewrite of the organizational model.

### Immediate consequence
Phase 1 work should prioritize reusable law, reusable profiles, and clean region-instance mapping.
