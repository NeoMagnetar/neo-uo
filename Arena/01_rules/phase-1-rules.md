# Phase 1 Rules

## Scope
The first milestone is the phase 1 enforcement shell only.

It is not a full MOBA. It is not a full league system. It is not a full arena game loop.

## Active phase 1 rules
- Region boundary awareness.
- No mounts inside the arena region.
- No recall inside the arena region.
- No gate travel inside the arena region.
- Entry handling.
- Exit handling.
- Rules messaging so players understand the restriction shell.

## Durable design goal
The durable asset is the reusable law layer that future modes will sit on.

## Deferred rule candidates
Future toggles may include no mark, no sacred journey, no pets, no summons, no outside healing, no looting, or custom death handling. These are not phase 1 commitments.
