# Codebase Audit

## Purpose

Track the main code and design areas likely to be affected by Neo UO’s early implementation work.

## Early Target Areas

- player death and continuity logic
- skill progression and training rates
- healing and wound-state behavior
- combat pacing and anti-zerg tuning
- tracking behavior and clue systems
- creature behavior and ecology adjustments
- item and storage rules
- housing / stronghold future extension points

## Immediate Technical Focus

The first implementation audit should identify likely touchpoints for:

- `PlayerMobile`
- death-related logic
- tracking systems
- progression and gain systems
- combat and damage recovery systems
- creature AI or pursuit behavior
