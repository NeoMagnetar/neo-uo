# Core Experience Manifesto

Neo UO should feel like a dangerous life, not a disposable match loop.

The player should enjoy being in the world:

- moving through it
- hunting through it
- gathering from it
- hiding within it
- preparing for the next encounter

Conflict should matter because it changes state.
A player may survive, but be wounded.
A player may escape, but remain hunted.
A player may win a fight, but create political consequences.
A player may lose without immediately dying, yet still face ransom, pursuit, or exposure.

Victory should often mean:

- surviving
- escaping
- protecting resources
- securing a rescue
- holding territory
- completing an extraction
- preserving a hero
- denying an enemy a clean outcome

Neo UO is not trying to maximize constant action.
It is trying to maximize meaningful tension.
