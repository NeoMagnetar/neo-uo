# Discussion Preservation Notes

## Purpose

This page exists to preserve important Neo UO discussion themes that should remain easy to find later without needing to scroll through old chat history.

It is a lightweight memory page, not a final system spec.

## Preserved Themes

### Survival-War Identity
Neo UO is aiming at a world where survival, tension, movement, and aftermath matter more than disposable kill-reset loops.

### Broader Character Lives
Long-lived characters may become broader, hardier, and more capable without mainly becoming raw delete engines.

### Backup Continuity
Players may maintain weaker backup characters so loss hurts without fully ejecting them from play.

### Wound and Recovery Thinking
Fights may leave lasting consequences, with stabilization happening earlier than full restoration.

### Tracking as Hunt
Tracking should create clues, trails, and pursuit rather than certainty arrows.

### Sparse but Dangerous Wilderness
The world may have fewer creatures overall, but each encounter may matter more.

### Predator / Prey / Patrol Behavior
Different creatures and groups may create distinct kinds of wilderness pressure.

### Carry Weight and Logistics
Supplies, burden, route choice, extraction, and hidden storage may become meaningful.

### Guild Heroes and Political Play
Guilds may eventually develop heroes, prisoners, rescues, famed items, and longer social memory.

### Arena as Related but Detached
Arena should broaden access without flattening world stakes.

## Usage Rule

When a future discussion introduces an idea that feels important but not yet ready for finalization, capture it in the relevant system page or here rather than leaving it only in chat.
