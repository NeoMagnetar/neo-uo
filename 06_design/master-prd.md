# Master PRD

## Executive Summary

Neo UO is a survival-war sandbox built on the Ultima Online chassis.

It is not a conventional shard with minor customizations or a simple hardcore ruleset layered over normal UO play. It is being designed as a distinct world where life has weight, movement has consequence, conflict leaves aftermath, and the world itself pressures the player through scarcity, travel, pursuit, politics, and social memory.

The game is intended to be:

- high consequence
- methodical
- open-world
- socially meaningful
- and compatible with both short and long play sessions

Neo UO should support two linked but distinct play lanes:

- a persistent survival-war world
- and a detached arena lane using the same combat grammar with lower stakes

## Product Vision

Create a UO-derived world where:

- surviving matters as much as killing
- defeat may become pursuit, wounds, ransom, or rescue rather than only instant reset
- characters, items, and guilds gain memory and political weight
- and players feel they are living in a dangerous war landscape rather than hopping between disposable encounters

## Vision Statement

Neo UO is an open-world survival-war sandbox built on the UO chassis, where life has weight, movement has consequence, and conflict often becomes a hunt rather than a quick reset fight.

## Product Positioning

Neo UO is not trying to:

- out-classic classic shards
- out-scale the largest servers
- function as a nostalgia trap
- or build a PvE-first theme park

Neo UO is trying to:

- offer a genuinely distinct UO experience
- serve as a destination shard for players seeking a different emotional model
- attract both UO veterans and players familiar with survival-world tension
- and create a world where social memory, geography, and survival matter

## Audience

### Primary Audience

- veteran UO players looking for a fresh ruleset
- hardcore sandbox and survival-minded players
- players who enjoy hunting, evasion, risk, scarcity, and emergent world stories

### Secondary Audience

- newer players drawn by arena mode or survival-world intrigue
- players who do not want to commit to all-day play
- players who may enter through lower-stakes combat before adopting the world lane

## Core Product Promise

Neo UO should deliver:

- meaningful life and death
- tense open-world movement
- slower, more deliberate combat
- incomplete recovery and lingering consequence
- stronger scarcity and resource choice
- guild politics and world memory over time
- a second mode that broadens access without diluting identity

## Core Experience Loop

The intended world loop is:

prepare -> roam -> acquire -> detect danger -> clash -> wound -> pursue or evade -> recover -> re-enter the world

The intended emotional structure is:

- ambient tension while moving through the world
- spikes of intense action when conflict arrives
- extended consequence after the clash
- real decisions about survival, recovery, extraction, or re-engagement

## Product Structure

### Lane 1 — Persistent Survival-War World

This is the main identity-defining mode.

Key traits:

- high consequence
- meaningful defeat
- slower progression
- dangerous-world reward acceleration
- tracking and hunts
- ecology pressure
- political and territorial expansion later
- long-term character and item significance

### Lane 2 — Detached Arena Mode

This is the secondary access and retention lane.

Key traits:

- shared combat grammar
- lower consequence
- practice and onboarding value
- variety and replayability
- separate from the world’s economy and death structure

## Non-Negotiable Design Principles

Neo UO must preserve the following:

- life must matter
- death must hurt without ejecting the player
- survival power must outscale raw burst power
- dangerous-world growth must outpace safe growth
- tracking must create hunts, not certainty
- groups must gain coverage more than linear dominance
- the world must create pressure without becoming exhausting clutter
- social consequence must matter beyond immediate kills

## System Pillars

### 1. Death and Continuity

Death matters. The player continues through weaker alternate lives.

### 2. Combat and Wounds

Combat should create aftermath rather than instant reset.

### 3. Tracking and Hunts

The player reads trails, movement, and risk rather than chasing certainty arrows.

### 4. Scarcity and Logistics

Gold, supplies, extraction, weight, and stashing all matter.

### 5. Wilderness Pressure

Predators, patrols, prey, burden, and movement shape travel as gameplay.

### 6. Politics and World Memory

Guild heroes, prisoners, rescues, grudges, and renowned items create social meaning.

## Progression Philosophy

Progression should feel:

- slower than disposable modern loops
- earned through exposure and risk
- deep enough for broad character development
- resistant to trivial macro-only mastery
- supportive of backup-character continuity without flattening loss

Long-lived characters should gain:

- resilience
- utility
- broader capability
- social value
- item and identity significance

They should not gain:

- unchecked delete power
- effortless dominance over everyone below them
- immunity to organized threat

## Combat Philosophy

Combat should be:

- tense
- readable
- slower than baseline burst PvP
- shaped by wounds and incomplete recovery
- capable of ending in survival, retreat, capture, or pursuit

The goal is not endless sponge combat.
The goal is lasting consequence.

## Economy Philosophy

The economy should favor:

- scarcity
- material choice
- meaningful gold
- logistics under pressure
- item significance
- future circulation pressure on important gear

Neo UO should avoid:

- endless disposable farming
- trivial banking and forgetting
- inflation-heavy progression loops

## Social Philosophy

Neo UO should support:

- guild identity
- heroes and survivors
- political captures
- rescues and ransoms
- notorious items
- remembered losses

The world should accumulate stories.

## MVP Scope

The MVP should prove the identity, not the full dream.

### In Scope

- local playable baseline
- early combat pacing direction
- early progression direction
- death / continuity foundation
- backup character philosophy
- tracking redesign prototype
- recovery / wound-state prototype
- ecology and scarcity direction
- repo and testing structure

### Out of Scope

- full capture politics
- full rescue gameplay
- full renowned-item circulation
- stronghold / siege completion
- deity system
- broad arena content expansion
- full late-game social systems

## Success Criteria

Neo UO is directionally successful if:

- players describe it as distinct from normal shards
- survival is meaningful
- death matters but does not fully eject the player
- combat creates aftermath
- travel and wilderness choices matter
- the world feels pressuring without feeling crowded
- the repo remains coherent enough to support real building and iteration

## Failure Conditions To Avoid

Neo UO should avoid becoming:

- standard shard PvP with permadeath stapled on
- a frustration simulator
- an empty world mistaken for a tense one
- a system pile with no center
- a design whose consequences are so severe players stop engaging

## Current Development Posture

The current project phase is structured pre-production moving toward local prototype iteration.

The immediate priority is to refine the design spine, preserve identity boundaries, and prepare the first implementation and local test passes around the most defining systems.
