# Master PRD

## Executive Summary

Neo UO is a survival-war sandbox built on the Ultima Online chassis.

It is not being designed as a standard shard with harsher penalties. It is being designed as a distinct world where life has weight, conflict has aftermath, and movement through the world carries real consequence.

The game is meant to support:

- meaningful character survival
- methodical open-world warfare
- slower, more consequential progression
- deeper political and social consequence
- a detached arena lane using the same combat grammar

## Product Vision

Create a UO-based world where:

- surviving matters as much as fighting
- defeat can lead to wounds, negotiation, or pursuit rather than immediate reset
- players value their characters, items, routes, and allies
- and both veterans and newcomers can be intrigued by the experience

## Product Positioning

Neo UO is:

- not trying to out-classic classic shards
- not trying to out-scale the largest servers
- not being built as a nostalgia trap
- not mainly a PvE treadmill

Neo UO is:

- a destination shard
- a survival-war world
- a high-consequence sandbox
- a system-rich alternative UO experience
- a project intended to become legible both internally and publicly

## Two-Lane Product Structure

### Persistent Survival-War World

The main world is high consequence and identity-defining.

Key traits:

- permadeath or character-retirement logic
- wounds and incomplete recovery
- hunting and evasion
- scarcity, logistics, and hidden storage decisions
- slower, meaningful progression
- bounty pressure and social consequence
- future territorial warfare

### Detached Arena Mode

A separate, lower-consequence combat lane.

Key traits:

- shared combat grammar with the world
- practice, onboarding, and retention value
- distinct from world economy and world death stakes
- useful to both veterans and newer players

## Core Experience

The intended loop is:

prepare -> roam -> acquire -> detect danger -> clash -> wound -> pursue or evade -> recover -> re-enter the world

Neo UO should feel like living a dangerous life in an active war landscape, not like queueing for disposable PvP resets.

## Gameplay Pillars

- life and death must matter
- survival and escape must be real victories
- hunts must matter, not just fights
- combat should be tense and drawn out enough to create aftermath
- the world should feel sparse but pressuring, not crowded and trivial
- characters should gain breadth, resilience, and utility more than raw deletion power
- social memory should matter: heroes, prisoners, famed items, grudges, rescues

## MVP Scope

The MVP should prove identity, not total content.

### In-Scope

- local playable baseline
- initial combat and progression tuning
- death / continuity doctrine prototype
- backup character philosophy
- tracking redesign prototype
- basic ecology and scarcity tuning assumptions
- documentation and testing structure

### Out-of-Scope

- full siege layer
- full deity system
- full political capture network
- full item renown system
- deep arena content breadth
- all future role-expression systems

## Success Criteria

Neo UO is directionally successful if:

- players describe it as tense, distinct, and memorable
- survival matters
- death hurts without ejecting the player
- travel and pursuit feel meaningful
- combat feels less disposable than stock UO
- the project maintains identity even at small scale
