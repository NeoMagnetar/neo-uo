# Roadmap

## Roadmap Philosophy

This roadmap is not a content wishlist. It is the staged path for proving Neo UO’s identity.

The early phases should answer:

- does the world feel different
- does death matter in the intended way
- does movement and pursuit matter
- and can the player stay engaged after loss

Only after those answers are directionally positive should deeper expansion systems be built.

## Phase 0 — Foundation and Documentation

### Goal

Create a stable project headquarters and a consistent design truth structure.

### Deliverables

- repo lane structure stabilized
- governance docs populated
- PRD and roadmap established
- core system pages established
- task and testing lanes initialized

### Success Condition

The repo becomes a usable planning headquarters instead of a loose note pile.

### Current State

In progress / largely established.

## Phase 1 — Playable Local Baseline

### Goal

Get a stable local Neo UO build running for real hands-on iteration.

### Deliverables

- local shard setup confirmed
- local testing workflow confirmed
- two-player test viability
- basic movement and combat sanity checks
- basic progression sanity checks

### Success Condition

Two people can reliably enter the world and test foundational play loops.

### Risks

- setup friction blocks iteration
- baseline UO assumptions hide system differences

## Phase 2 — Death, Continuity, and Progression Core

### Goal

Prove that Neo UO can make death meaningful without collapsing player continuation.

### Deliverables

- early death-routing design
- continuity model for backup characters
- safe vs dangerous training doctrine turned into prototype rules
- first recovery / wound-state prototype or narrow test model
- account-level anti-abuse boundaries drafted

### Success Condition

Death hurts, but the player still has a reason to keep playing.

### Risks

- continuity becomes too strong and cheapens loss
- loss becomes too final and drives disengagement

## Phase 3 — Hunt and Combat Identity

### Goal

Make conflict and movement feel like Neo UO instead of default UO.

### Deliverables

- tracking prototype
- anti-zerg spell-pressure prototype or tuning model
- drawn-out combat pass
- wound / incomplete recovery pass
- burden / mobility concept pass
- early ecology pressure pass

### Success Condition

A fight can meaningfully turn into a pursuit, retreat, or survival moment.

### Risks

- combat becomes spongey
- tracking becomes too vague or too precise
- travel pressure becomes tedium

## Phase 4 — Closed Playtest

### Goal

Test the doctrine against real player behavior.

### Deliverables

- small controlled playtester set
- structured feedback capture
- first external balance findings
- documented tension vs frustration findings

### Success Condition

The project learns what players actually experience instead of only what the design intends.

### Risks

- testers interpret systems using baseline UO expectations
- feedback is too unstructured to guide iteration

## Phase 5 — Arena Mode Prototype

### Goal

Create the first detached low-stakes combat lane.

### Deliverables

- first arena ruleset
- clear separation rules from the world lane
- practice / onboarding value
- shared combat grammar proof

### Success Condition

Arena mode is fun and attractive without contaminating the world lane.

### Risks

- arena becomes balance master for the world
- arena rewards flatten the importance of the world

## Phase 6 — World Expansion Systems

### Goal

Deepen the sandbox once the foundation is proven.

### Deliverables

- stronger PvE reward differentiation
- hidden stash logic
- bounty system growth
- item renown direction
- early capture / rescue structural design
- broader role-expression expansion

### Success Condition

The world gains depth without losing center.

### Risks

- systems sprawl
- too many unfinished partial mechanics weaken clarity

## Phase 7 — Strongholds and Siege Layer

### Goal

Make territory and defended space politically meaningful.

### Deliverables

- attackable stronghold logic
- proxy defense concepts
- breach / defense / repair rules
- territory pressure model
- future tower-defense experiments

### Success Condition

Territory becomes a meaningful conflict layer instead of decorative housing.

### Risks

- siege logic becomes noisy and abusive
- territory rules overwhelm smaller-scale survival play

## Cross-Phase Rules

### Rule 1

Do not implement deep late-game politics before proving the basic death / continuity foundation.

### Rule 2

Do not implement full stronghold systems before movement, scarcity, and wilderness pressure are directionally good.

### Rule 3

Do not let arena work derail the main-world identity pass.

### Rule 4

Whenever in doubt, deepen the project center before adding a new outer system.

## Immediate Next Focus

The current immediate next focus after design-spine completion should be:

- local prototype readiness
- first implementation-area mapping
- first implementation candidates for death, progression, and tracking
- structured test planning for early identity systems
