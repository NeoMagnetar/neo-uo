# Light Travel Loadouts

## Purpose
Helps the repo track armor kits built around speed, range, movement, and survival while traveling.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally light, practical, and built around getting in and out alive rather than holding a line.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important for wilderness movement, logistics, and long-range survival identity.
