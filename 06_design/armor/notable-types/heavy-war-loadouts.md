# Heavy War Loadouts

## Purpose
Helps the repo track armor packages built for conflict presence, staying power, and committed battle roles.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally heavier, slower, and more suited to taking or holding punishment.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important for war-presence design and for separating defense from mobility-focused kits.

## Experimental Directions
- Review how heavy kits should trade movement for commitment and staying power.
- Explore what heavy-war readiness should cost in logistics and pursuit.

## Related Families
chain-armor, plate-armor, shields-and-defensive-equipment

## Status
Status: preserved for design and future testing.
