# Prestige and Renowned Gear Context

## Purpose
Helps the repo track armor as a social, political, or prestige object rather than only as pure combat gear.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally tied to fame, rarity, trophy value, or social recognition more than pure battlefield efficiency alone.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important for later discussions about circulation, prestige, anti-hoarding, and world memory.

## Experimental Directions
- Review how famous gear should circulate or stick in the world.
- Explore social value without assuming every prestige item must be mechanically dominant.
