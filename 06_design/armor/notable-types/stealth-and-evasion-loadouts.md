# Stealth and Evasion Loadouts

## Purpose
Helps track armor choices that support concealment, low burden, and escape-oriented play.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally low-burden and low-profile, favoring movement and survival over brute mitigation.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important for wounded survival, hunt evasion, and stealth-heavy role identity.

## Experimental Directions
- Review how low-burden armor should support concealment and escape.
- Explore whether stealth kits should be materially worse at prolonged stand-up war.
