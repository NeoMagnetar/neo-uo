# Wilderness Survival Loadouts

## Purpose
Helps track hybrid field-use armor philosophy for players who expect travel, hunting, and practical danger rather than clean duels.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally mixed and practical, balancing protection, mobility, and carry burden.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important because Neo UO places strong value on dangerous-world survival and field practicality.

## Experimental Directions
- Review hybrid survival kits as practical equipment, not only stat compromises.
- Explore how burden, supply, and route risk should affect armor choice.

## Related Families
leather-armor, studded-and-reinforced-light-armor, chain-armor, cloth-and-non-armor-context

## Status
Status: preserved for design and future testing.
