# Armor Design Principles

Neo UO should think about armor as survivability with tradeoffs, not as flat protection sameness.

## Current principles
- Survivability should come with real tradeoff.
- Identity should matter more than flat sameness.
- Burden and mobility relevance should remain part of the design.
- Not every armor should feel equally optimal everywhere.
- Lighter vs heavier kit should change how the world feels.

## Constraint
This layer preserves direction and questions. It does not finalize tuning.
