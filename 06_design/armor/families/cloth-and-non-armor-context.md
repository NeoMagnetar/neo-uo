# Cloth and Non-Armor Context

## Baseline UO Role
Clothing, robes, and low-protection or non-armor wearables that traditionally offer little direct defense.

## Era / Expansion Relevance
Base-UO dominant context that remains relevant even if later systems add more specialized armor interactions.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important because vulnerability, mobility, and social or caster presentation should remain meaningful choices in Neo UO.

## Experimental Directions
- Review whether low protection should grant real mobility or logistics advantages.
- Explore whether vulnerability should be a deliberate world-play choice rather than a beginner accident.
- Keep room for traveler, caster, and noncombat identity without forcing equal safety.
