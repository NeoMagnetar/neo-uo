# Plate Armor

## Baseline UO Role
Heavy protection with high commitment, strong survivability, and clear burden implications.

## Era / Expansion Relevance
Base-UO dominant family and one of the clearest anchors for armor tradeoff thinking.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important because heavy protection should shape survival, pursuit, retreat, and presence in a survival-war game.

## Experimental Directions
- Review plate as war presence and commitment, not simply best-in-slot defense.
- Explore whether heavier protection should slow pursuit and escape meaningfully.
- Test how plate interacts with burden, logistics, and field endurance.
