# Armor Balance Philosophy

Neo UO should review armor through a wider world lens, not only through simple mitigation comparisons.

## Review lenses
- Survivability value.
- Mobility and pursuit cost.
- Burden and logistics cost.
- Stealth and evasion interaction.
- Crafting and rarity relevance.
- Arena/world separation where relevant.
- Role identity and counterplay.

## Constraint
This file does not finalize armor balance. It records how future balance review should be framed.
