# Weapon Balance Philosophy

Neo UO should review weapons through a wider world lens, not only through simple throughput comparisons.

## Review lenses
- Lethality vs survivability.
- Chase and escape implications.
- Burden and logistics implications.
- Group-vs-solo usefulness.
- Arena/world separation where relevant.
- Role identity and counterplay.

## Constraint
This file does not finalize weapon balance. It records how future balance review should be framed.
