# Staves and Arcane-Adjacent Weapons

## Purpose
Helps the repo track mage-adjacent or utility-combat weapon identities without assuming they are already fully supported.

## Baseline Identity
Traditionally occupy a hybrid space between melee, support, or arcane flavor depending on era and system context.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important for later hybrid ideas and for keeping weapon identity open around support or occult play.

## Experimental Directions
- Review whether staves should signal defensive, support, or arcane-adjacent roles.
- Explore whether hybrid weapons should matter more in world play than arena play.
