# Weapon Design Principles

Neo UO should think about weapons as identity carriers, not as flat bundles of numbers.

## Current principles
- Identity should matter more than stat sameness.
- Weapons should support a survival-war role, not only clean duel math.
- Burden and carry relevance should remain part of the conversation.
- Not every weapon should feel equally optimal everywhere.
- Later-era additions are allowed if they support the design and do not erase the broader world identity.

## Constraint
This layer preserves direction and questions. It does not finalize tuning.
