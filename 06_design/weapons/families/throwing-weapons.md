# Throwing Weapons

## Baseline UO Role
Represents later-era ranged combat through thrown weapons with its own feel and handling expectations.

## Era / Expansion Relevance
Stygian Abyss / later-era family and not assumed to be a core baseline for Neo UO.

## Neo UO Relevance
Important as an experimentation target because it offers ranged pressure with a different identity than archery.

## Experimental Directions
- Preserve as an experimental lane rather than assuming it belongs at the center of the game.
- Review how throwing differs from archery in burden, cadence, and combat commitment.
- Test whether it supports skirmish or specialist roles better than broad baseline play.
