# Tracking and Hunts

## Purpose

Turn pursuit into a real gameplay layer.

## Player Experience Goal

Tracking should feel like reading a trail, not following a GPS arrow.

## Doctrine

- remove certainty-driven arrow tracking
- provide directional or location-based evidence instead
- clue quality should decay over time
- hunts should reward map knowledge, inference, and persistence
- injury, burden, and recent movement may affect trace quality later

## Open Questions

- how precise should tracking clues be?
- how long should tracks persist?
- what counter-tracking tools should exist?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
