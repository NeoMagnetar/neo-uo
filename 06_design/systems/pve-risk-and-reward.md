# PvE Risk and Reward

## Purpose

Define why monsters matter without making Neo UO a PvE-first game.

## Player Experience Goal

PvE should create risk sites, resource pressure, and conflict magnets.

## Doctrine

- monsters matter, but do not define the game’s identity
- soloable and group-demanding encounters should both exist
- unique rewards should favor utility, specialization, and survival value more than raw oppressive burst
- PvE should expose players to danger rather than isolate them from it

## Open Questions

- which rewards should be unique, tradable, or circulating?
- what PvE difficulty split should exist between lone and group encounters?
- how much world scarcity supports the right tension?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
