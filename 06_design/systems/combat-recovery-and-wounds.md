# Combat Recovery and Wounds

## Purpose

Define how injury and incomplete recovery shape the aftermath of conflict.

## Player Experience Goal

A player should often survive a fight while still carrying meaningful consequences from it.

## Doctrine

- distinguish immediate HP from longer-term wound states
- allow stabilization before full restoration
- deep recovery should take time, safety, or resources
- recovery should matter without turning sessions into hostage waiting
- escaping a fight should save a life, not erase the fight

## Open Questions

- what is the target stabilization window?
- what is the target full-recovery window?
- what wound penalties are most meaningful without being excessive?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
