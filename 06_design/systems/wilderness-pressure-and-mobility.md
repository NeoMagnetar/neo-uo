# Wilderness Pressure and Mobility

## Purpose

Make travel itself part of gameplay.

## Player Experience Goal

Movement through the world should require route judgment, load management, and awareness of layered threats.

## Doctrine

- predators, patrols, and prey should have distinct behaviors
- burden and terrain should affect escape and pursuit
- some threats should pressure players over distance without making escape impossible
- route choice should matter because fleeing one danger may create another

## Open Questions

- how persistent should apex pursuit be?
- how should burden affect speed and fatigue?
- how should territorial spillover be signaled to players?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
