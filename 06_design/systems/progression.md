# Progression

## Purpose

Define how players become more capable over time.

## Player Experience Goal

Progression should feel meaningful, slower than disposable modern loops, and weighted toward dangerous play.

## Doctrine

- dangerous-world gain should exceed safe-zone gain
- safe training may exist, but at materially inferior rates
- not every skill should be equally unattended-trainable
- broader, more resilient characters are desirable
- mastery should take time

## Open Questions

- what safe-vs-dangerous gain ratio feels healthy?
- which skills require active context?
- how far above the baseline skill cap should Neo UO eventually go?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
