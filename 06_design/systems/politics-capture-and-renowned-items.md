# Politics, Capture, and Renowned Items

## Purpose

Define how guild politics, social prestige, capture states, and notable items create world memory.

## Player Experience Goal

Characters, guilds, and items should become socially meaningful rather than privately disposable.

## Doctrine

- some defeats should lead to leverage, not just death
- capture should be a state, not forced live imprisonment
- rescue, ransom, exchange, and execution should all be conceptually available
- some items should be politically visible and difficult to bury forever
- heroes, relics, prisoners, and guild grudges should matter to the world

## Open Questions

- which targets can be captured?
- what resolves capture?
- how should renowned items remain in circulation or social visibility?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
