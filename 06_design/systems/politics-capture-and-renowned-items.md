# Politics, Capture, and Renowned Items

## Purpose

Define how Neo UO creates social memory through guild politics, hero characters, defeat leverage, rescue play, and meaningful item circulation.

This system is where the project begins to feel like a living political world instead of only a combat ruleset.

## Player Experience Goal

The player should feel that:

- guilds matter
- heroes matter
- important defeats matter
- rescue and ransom matter
- and certain items and characters become socially recognized parts of the world

## Design Position

Neo UO should support defeat states and world objects that generate leverage, not just simple kill resolution.

The world should remember:

- important characters
- notable items
- major captures
- famous rescues
- guild feuds
- politically meaningful losses

## Core Doctrine

### Law 1 — Not Every Important Defeat Should End in Immediate Death

Some defeats should create leverage, negotiation, and follow-on play.

### Law 2 — Capture Must Be a State, Not Forced Live Imprisonment

A captured player should not need to remain actively trapped in real-time gameplay to preserve the fiction.

### Law 3 — Heroes and Renowned Items Must Remain World-Relevant

Famous characters and significant items should not simply disappear into private safety forever.

## Guild Hero Doctrine

Neo UO should naturally allow guilds to produce:

- champions
- trackers
- crafters
- scouts
- leaders
- relic holders
- political prisoners
- legendary survivors

The world becomes richer when some lives matter socially, not just numerically.

## Capture Doctrine

Capture is conceptually attractive because it creates:

- ransom
- diplomacy
- rescue
- leverage
- execution risk
- guild mobilization

This is better than death-only resolution in many important cases.

### Capture Must Not Become

- forced idle imprisonment
- timezone punishment
- real-world scheduling hostility
- pure grief leverage

### Preferred Model

Capture should be represented as a character state:

- the character is unavailable for normal deployment
- the player may continue on another character
- the captor gains leverage
- the victim side gains urgency
- the situation resolves by ransom, rescue, exchange, execution, or timer consequence

## Rescue Doctrine

Rescue gameplay is one of the strongest long-term social systems in Neo UO.

It creates:

- guild mobilization
- role value for backups
- real geography relevance
- meaningful political escalation

This should only be added once the underlying continuity and defeat systems are stable.

## Renowned Item Doctrine

Some items should become more than stat sticks.

They should become:

- recognized
- desirable
- historically meaningful
- politically visible
- worth hunting for, trading for, or ransoming over

## Anti-Hoarding Doctrine

The goal is not to ban storage arbitrarily.
The goal is to stop world-significant items from vanishing permanently into untouchable safety.

The healthier long-term model is circulation pressure, not crude prohibition.

### Possible Circulation Pressure Types Later

- notoriety
- maintenance
- exposure requirements
- ownership visibility
- political interest
- weakened dormancy if never used

The exact method is later work.

## MVP Version

The MVP should not try to build the full capture-politics network immediately.

However, the repo should preserve the doctrine now so future systems do not accidentally contradict it.

### MVP Goal

Hold the social design line early, even if implementation comes much later.

## Later Expansion Version

Later phases may add:

- capture-state resolution windows
- ransom interface or systems
- rescue operations
- prisoner exchange logic
- execution outcomes
- renowned item visibility
- circulation pressure rules
- guild prestige and notoriety systems

## Design Risks

### Risk: Hostage-State Design Failure

If capture requires the player to remain effectively idle, it will feel punishing rather than dramatic.

Mitigation:

- captured-state logic instead of live imprisonment
- alternate-character continuation
- multiple resolution paths

### Risk: Political Systems Become Noise

If too many names, items, or feuds are “special,” nothing feels special.

Mitigation:

- keep significance rare
- let fame emerge, not only be assigned

### Risk: Anti-Hoarding Feels Arbitrary

If important items are restricted clumsily, the system may feel unfair.

Mitigation:

- favor circulation pressure and exposure over absolute restrictions

## Implementation Notes

Do not implement this system early at full depth.
It should follow after:

- death / continuity rules
- backup-character support
- stronger world identity
- some shared guild-level play behavior

## Open Questions

- who can be captured and under what conditions?
- what are the healthiest capture resolution windows?
- how should rescue be staged?
- which items qualify as renowned?
- what circulation pressure mechanisms feel systemic rather than arbitrary?

## Current Direction

Neo UO should eventually support social memory through hero lives, political defeats, rescues, and world-significant items that remain visible in the game world.
