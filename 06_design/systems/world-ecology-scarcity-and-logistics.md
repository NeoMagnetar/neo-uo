# World Ecology, Scarcity, and Logistics

## Purpose

Define how Neo UO makes the wilderness feel sparse, pressuring, and logistically meaningful rather than crowded and disposable.

This system governs encounter density, scarcity, gold pressure, carrying choices, field storage, and extraction tension.

## Player Experience Goal

The player should feel that:

- the world is quieter than a standard shard
- what they find matters more
- what they carry matters more
- and every trip into the wilderness involves judgment rather than autopilot farming

## Design Position

Neo UO should prefer ambient tension over ambient clutter.

There should be fewer meaningless encounters and fewer endless resource loops. Remaining encounters should matter more.

## Core Doctrine

### Law 1 — Lower Density Must Not Mean Lower Pressure

Reducing creature count should not make the world feel empty.

### Law 2 — Scarcity Must Produce Decisions

Gold, resources, and supplies should be limited enough that choices matter.

### Law 3 — Carrying and Stashing Must Matter

The player should not always be able to extract or store everything safely and immediately.

## Ecology Philosophy

The wilderness should feel like:

- a space of intermittent but real danger
- a place where meaningful encounters happen
- an environment where territory, route, and behavior matter

It should not feel like:

- a constant combat treadmill
- a respawn carpet
- a resource vacuum

## Creature Density Philosophy

Neo UO should reduce:

- constant filler mobs
- endless chains of disposable combat
- inflated background noise

Neo UO should increase:

- encounter significance
- regional identity
- predator behavior
- patrol pressure
- resource tension

## Scarcity Philosophy

Gold and resources should matter more than they do in disposable loops.

This means:

- reduced gold inflation
- stronger material choice pressure
- more meaningful stash decisions
- more meaningful extraction decisions
- more meaningful losses

## Hidden Stash Doctrine

Hidden stashes or field caches are a strong fit for Neo UO, but they must not become private invulnerable banking.

### Intended Use

- temporary offloading
- emergency concealment
- delayed extraction
- tactical resource survival

### What They Must Not Become

- perfect safety
- unlimited hidden wealth storage
- no-risk world banking

### Long-Term Healthy Model

Stashes should be:

- limited
- local
- discoverable
- decaying or vulnerable in some form
- useful, but not absolute

## Carry Weight Philosophy

Lower carry freedom supports:

- meaningful preparation
- harder route choices
- supply discipline
- differentiated roles
- better extraction tension

This system must be tuned carefully so that it creates pressure, not constant annoyance.

## Experimental Directions

The following ideas have been discussed and should stay visible for future exploration:

### Lower-Density World Variants

- drastically fewer animals and monsters overall
- fewer but more meaningful encounters
- less constant engagement, more ambient risk

### Harder Individual Encounter Meaning

- a grizzly bear or apex creature should feel like a real event
- a small pack should be a tactical problem, not filler
- some creatures may be intentionally not worth fighting unless the player is ready

### Resource and Gold Tightening

- gold should feel reduced and meaningful
- supplies should require real choice
- transport and storage decisions should create tension

### Hidden Stash Possibilities

- players may stash valuables in the field if they cannot safely extract
- those stashes should still carry discovery risk
- field storage should support survival, not erase consequence

### Encounter Significance Over Farming Volume

- training should come from more meaningful, slower, riskier interactions
- the world should encourage deliberate engagement instead of chain-kill loops

These remain exploratory tuning and system-space directions.

## MVP Version

The MVP should likely include:

- reduced density assumptions
- early scarcity tuning
- lower gold abundance direction
- early carry-discipline thinking
- no fully realized stash system yet unless very easy to prototype

### MVP Goal

Make the world feel less noisy and more deliberate.

## Later Expansion Version

Later versions may add:

- discoverable field caches
- dynamic stash logic
- stronger region identity
- better economic scarcity systems
- rare supply chains
- later social or territorial pressure on extraction routes

## Design Risks

### Risk: Empty-World Failure

The world feels dead instead of tense.

Mitigation:

- preserve behavioral pressure
- use stronger encounter identity
- keep roads, regions, and predators meaningful

### Risk: Scarcity Becomes Chore

Players may feel starved without feeling engaged.

Mitigation:

- tie scarcity to meaningful choice, not only deprivation
- preserve opportunities for gain and improvisation

### Risk: Stashes Break the Economy

Field caches may become easy hoarding tools.

Mitigation:

- keep them risky, local, limited, and contestable

## Implementation Notes

Start with the simplest version:

- less overall density
- less disposable gold flow
- clearer encounter meaning

Do not build the full logistics simulation before the basic world loop is fun.

## Open Questions

- what creature density preserves tension without emptiness?
- how much gold reduction is healthy?
- what carry-weight pressure feels meaningful?
- how should hidden stashes be created, found, limited, or decayed?
- which resources should become strategically important first?

## Current Direction

Neo UO should create a sparse but dangerous wilderness where carrying, hiding, and extracting matter as much as finding.
