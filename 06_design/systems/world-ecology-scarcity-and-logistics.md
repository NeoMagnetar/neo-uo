# World Ecology, Scarcity, and Logistics

## Purpose

Define how the world stays pressuring without becoming crowded or noisy.

## Player Experience Goal

The wilderness should feel sparse but meaningful, where encounters and carried resources actually matter.

## Doctrine

- reduce ambient clutter, not ambient tension
- fewer creatures can still create more pressure if behavior and placement matter
- resource scarcity should make carrying, hiding, and extraction meaningful
- hidden stashes may exist as survival tools, but must not become consequence-free banking

## Open Questions

- what density feels tense rather than empty?
- what stash rules feel risky enough?
- how strong should carry-weight limitations be?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
