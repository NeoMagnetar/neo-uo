# Combat Systems

## Purpose

Define the overall combat identity for Neo UO.

## Player Experience Goal

Combat should be more deliberate, less disposable, and more likely to create aftermath.

## Doctrine

- fights should resolve more slowly than stock burst PvP
- survivability should rise more readily than raw kill pressure
- group stacking should face efficiency pressure
- combat should create states like wounded, pursued, exposed, or forced to retreat
- a fight should often continue as a hunt after the initial clash

## Open Questions

- what exact TTK shift feels right?
- how much damage reduction versus health increase is healthiest?
- which combat roles need the earliest attention?
