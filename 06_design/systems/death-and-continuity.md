# Death and Continuity

## Purpose

Define how death matters without ejecting the player from the game.

## Player Experience Goal

A character death should feel serious and memorable, but the player should retain meaningful continuity.

## Doctrine

- character death must matter
- backup character structures should soften total restart paralysis
- PvP defeat may sometimes create negotiation or capture layers instead of immediate death
- continuity must not trivialize death
- demotion is acceptable; total ejection should be avoided

## Open Questions

- what is the right number of developing backup characters?
- how should retirement versus death be handled at account level?
- when should capture be available instead of direct death?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
