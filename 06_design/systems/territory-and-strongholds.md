# Territory and Strongholds

## Purpose

Define the long-term path from housing toward meaningful territorial conflict.

## Player Experience Goal

Bases and defended areas should become strategic assets, not passive storage shells.

## Doctrine

- housing should eventually matter politically
- strongholds should become defendable and contestable
- siege logic should be readable and not pure chaos
- later proxy-based defensive structures may be used if they fit the engine better than full rework

## Open Questions

- what can be damaged?
- how are defenders alerted?
- what is temporary destruction versus permanent loss?

## Current Status

Future-oriented doctrine established.
