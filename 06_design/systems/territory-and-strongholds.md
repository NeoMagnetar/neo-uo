# Territory and Strongholds

## Purpose

Define the long-term path by which Neo UO turns space, housing, and defended locations into meaningful strategic and political assets.

This is a later-phase system, but it needs early doctrinal clarity so the project does not accidentally lock itself into purely decorative housing logic.

## Player Experience Goal

The player should feel that:
- where groups live matters,
- defended space matters,
- territory changes how conflict unfolds,
- and guild presence in the world becomes materially real.

## Design Position

Neo UO should eventually move beyond housing as simple storage or vanity.
Strongholds should become:
- meaningful locations,
- defensible assets,
- political targets,
- and later conflict generators.

## Core Territory Doctrine

### Law 1 — Territory Must Matter More Than Decoration
Space should change behavior, preparation, defense, and conflict.

### Law 2 — Defended Structures Must Create Meaningful Choices
Strongholds should not be invulnerable private worlds, but they also should not be disposable chaos magnets.

### Law 3 — Territory Systems Must Follow the Project Center
Strongholds should be built only after survival, movement, and world pressure are already directionally strong.

## Stronghold Philosophy

A stronghold is not just a house.
It is a place where:
- storage matters
- defense matters
- attack planning matters
- response timing matters
- political significance can accumulate

## Planned Direction

The project direction includes the idea that some housing or defended areas may eventually be attackable.

This should be approached as:
- a later-phase extension
- a readable ruleset
- a controlled objective system
- not a full destructive free-for-all at first

## Proxy Defense Direction

A promising long-term approach is to use entities such as NPC defenders, stationed elements, or other proxy structures to simulate fortification logic without requiring immediate full engine rework.

This direction is attractive because it may:
- reduce initial implementation cost
- preserve readability
- allow attackable defense points
- create siege objectives without rewriting everything

This idea must be carefully shaped so it feels intentional rather than arbitrary.

## Territory Gameplay Value

A territory system should eventually create:
- guild mobilization
- reasons to defend or raid
- strategic storage pressure
- attack timing decisions
- rescue or extraction complications
- political leverage and retaliation

## What Territory Must Not Become

- pure grief architecture
- unreadable permanent destruction noise
- constant off-hours punishment
- a system so punishing that small groups cannot participate at all

## Experimental Directions

The following discussed ideas should remain preserved here:

### Attackable Housing Direction
- player strongholds may eventually become vulnerable or contestable
- storage and safety may become political questions instead of permanent absolutes
- defended space should matter without becoming pure destruction spam

### Proxy Wall / NPC Defense Concepts
- NPCs, vendors, guards, or other stand-in structures may be used as defense nodes
- this may allow fortification gameplay without a full housing rewrite
- the design should feel intentional and readable, not hacky or arbitrary

### Prison and Holding-Space Concepts
- important captured characters may eventually be tied to defended places
- castles, compounds, or holding areas may create rescue or leverage stories
- this should remain fictionally rich without becoming real-time player hostage scheduling

### Stronghold as Political Asset
- a guild base may later matter for more than storage
- it may become part of rescue, defense, retaliation, or status
- losing or threatening it may become a political event

### Later Tower-Defense Flavor
- there may eventually be defense layers that feel somewhat like tower-defense or staged fortification play
- this is a later exploratory direction, not a current build commitment

These are preserved concepts, not final territory mechanics.

## MVP Version

Territory and strongholds are not MVP systems.

### MVP Goal
Preserve the doctrine and future direction now so later implementation fits the project identity.

## Early Pre-Implementation Questions

Before this system becomes active, the project will need answers to:
- what exactly can be attacked?
- when can it be attacked?
- how are defenders notified?
- what damage is temporary?
- what loss is permanent?
- how does this interact with storage, stashes, and logistics?
- how does it avoid becoming oppressive for smaller groups?

## Later Expansion Version

Later versions may include:
- attackable strongholds
- limited siege windows or pressure models
- proxy-defense entities
- repair and rebuild loops
- territory-linked storage pressure
- road and route pressure tied to guild presence

## Not Yet Canonized

The following are intentionally unresolved:
- exact attack window rules
- exact storage vulnerability rules
- exact proxy-defense implementation model
- exact rescue / prison interaction with territory
- exact scale assumptions for solo vs guild participation

## Design Risks

### Risk: Siege Noise
If territory conflict is always on and poorly bounded, the system can feel exhausting and abusive.

Mitigation:
- use readable rules
- delay until the center is proven
- define attack pressure clearly

### Risk: Decorative Irrelevance
If housing never becomes more than storage, the project loses a major future dimension.

Mitigation:
- preserve design space now
- do not lock thinking into passive housing assumptions

### Risk: Only Large Groups Matter
If territory systems scale only for dominant groups, the world narrows too much.

Mitigation:
- think about proportional pressure
- keep smaller-scale participation meaningful

## Open Questions

- what is the first believable stronghold objective?
- how should proxy defenses work?
- what is the first safe prototype that does not require full siege architecture?
- how are defenders informed and how much response time is healthy?
- what role should territory play in politics, storage, or prisoner systems later?

## Current Direction

Territory and strongholds should become a later-phase strategic layer that turns space and defended presence into meaningful parts of Neo UO’s political world.
