# Arena Mode

## Purpose

Define the detached low-stakes combat lane.

## Player Experience Goal

The arena should attract play, teach combat language, and remain meaningfully separate from the main world.

## Doctrine

- arena and world share combat grammar
- arena and world do not share survival stakes
- arena must not bypass world progression truths
- arena can serve onboarding, practice, and variety functions

## Open Questions

- what is the first arena format?
- what rewards, if any, should remain purely cosmetic or separate?
- how does the arena support rather than dilute Neo UO identity?

## Current Status

Initial doctrine established.
