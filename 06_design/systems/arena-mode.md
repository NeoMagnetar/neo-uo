# Arena Mode

## Purpose

Define the detached lower-stakes combat lane and protect Neo UO from mixing its world identity with its access-and-retention lane.

## Player Experience Goal

Arena mode should:

- be fun on its own
- teach combat grammar
- give players a lower-risk way to engage
- attract both veterans and curious newcomers
- remain clearly distinct from the main world

## Design Position

Arena mode is a support lane, not the identity core of Neo UO.

It exists to:

- broaden access
- create replayable combat
- give players something to do when they do not want to risk a world character
- create a possible on-ramp into the survival world

It must not become the source of truth for world balance, world stakes, or world progression.

## Core Arena Doctrine

### Law 1 — Shared Combat Grammar, Different Stakes

Arena and world modes should feel related in mechanics but not identical in emotional context.

### Law 2 — Arena Must Not Trivialize the World

Arena rewards and systems must not bypass world stakes, progression, or scarcity.

### Law 3 — Arena Can Teach, But Must Not Replace the World

Arena should make the main game more legible, not make the world unnecessary.

## Arena Value Proposition

Arena mode gives Neo UO:

- a practice space
- a retention tool
- a low-friction entry point
- a fun repeatable mode
- a bridge for players who are intrigued by the combat but not yet ready for full world stakes

## Intended Relationship To The World

### Shared

- basic movement language
- combat timing language
- ability familiarity
- visual / thematic coherence

### Separate

- survival stakes
- world death and continuity
- world economy
- world logistics
- world social leverage
- world scarcity meaning

## Experimental Directions

The following ideas are part of the preserved Neo UO design space:

### Arena as a Hook

- arena may attract players who are not yet ready for the survival world
- arena may keep existing world players engaged on lower-risk sessions
- arena may serve as a bridge between unfamiliar players and Neo UO’s core combat grammar

### Shared Ability Language

- some later combat additions may appear in both arena and world
- the feel of fighting should remain related across both modes
- the stakes, economy, and persistence should remain separate

### LoL-Inspired Direction

- later arena formats may borrow from structured battleground or lane-based inspiration
- this is exploratory, not current MVP commitment
- any such mode should still feel like Neo UO rather than a disconnected imported game

### Broader Retention Function

- some players may visit Neo UO for arena sessions while remaining only part-time world players
- that is acceptable if the two modes reinforce rather than erase each other

These ideas are preserved as real part of the project’s intended horizon.

## First Arena Version

The first arena version should be intentionally narrow.

### First Arena Objectives

- prove fun
- prove readability
- preserve separation
- preserve shared combat feel

### First Arena Should Not Try To Solve

- full esports balance
- broad competitive ranking ecosystem
- all future minigames at once
- progression entanglement with world mode

## Potential Future Role

Arena mode may later serve as:

- onboarding
- side-play for current world players
- combat literacy training
- a space for LoL-inspired experimental formats
- community retention during low-risk sessions

## Design Risks

### Risk: Arena Contamination

Arena systems begin dictating world expectations.

Mitigation:

- preserve separation rules
- explicitly document what never transfers

### Risk: Arena Rewards Flatten World Stakes

If arena gives too much meaningful progression or economy value, world risk loses importance.

Mitigation:

- keep rewards separate, cosmetic, limited, or carefully bounded

### Risk: Arena Feels Unrelated

If it becomes too detached in feel, it stops helping the broader project.

Mitigation:

- preserve shared combat grammar and readable lineage

## MVP Version

Arena mode is not a first-core MVP requirement.
However, its separation doctrine should be documented early so future implementation does not drift.

### MVP Goal

Hold the boundary now, implement later.

## Later Expansion Version

Later versions may include:

- first duel or team format
- small scenario-based combat
- experimental role-expression mode
- later LoL-inspired battleground structures

## Open Questions

- what is the first minimal arena format?
- what reward model is healthy?
- how much customization should be shared from the world?
- what part of combat should feel identical, and what should feel simplified?

## Current Direction

Arena mode should broaden access and preserve engagement while staying clearly secondary to the survival-war world.
