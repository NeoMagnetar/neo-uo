# Glossary

## Neo UO

The project name for this survival-war sandbox built on the Ultima Online chassis.

## Survival-War Sandbox

A world where survival, movement, logistics, scarcity, and conflict matter together rather than existing as separate activity lanes.

## Combat Grammar

The shared language of movement, timing, ability use, threat response, and counterplay that should remain recognizable across both world and arena modes.

## Ambient Tension

Background pressure created by risk, scarcity, unclear threat, and meaningful movement rather than constant direct action.

## Ambient Clutter

Low-value constant world activity that creates noise without meaningful pressure.

## Death and Continuity

The design area concerned with making character death meaningful while preserving player continuation through weaker or developing alternate lives.

## Backup Character

A secondary or developing character whose main purpose is to preserve continuity after the loss of a stronger main.

## Wound State

A lingering injury condition that affects readiness beyond immediate visible HP loss.

## Stabilization

The short-term process of recovering enough to remain playable without being fully restored.

## Full Restoration

The return to real battle-readiness after deeper recovery.

## Tracking by Evidence

A tracking model that gives directional or temporal clues instead of certainty-based target arrows.

## Hunt Loop

The sequence of trace, infer, move, reacquire, predict, and risk contact.

## Sparse World Pressure

A world condition where lower encounter density still creates meaningful tension because behavior, scarcity, and geography matter.

## Logistics

The practical layer of carrying, storing, moving, hiding, and extracting valuables and supplies.

## Hidden Stash / Field Cache

A world-storage concept meant for temporary concealment or delayed extraction, not perfect invulnerable banking.

## Burden / Encumbrance

The movement and decision pressure created by carrying more equipment, loot, or supplies.

## Territorial Spillover

A world condition where fleeing one danger may force a player into a second danger, making geography strategically meaningful.

## Anti-Zerg Pressure

Any system that weakens dense, mindless group efficiency while preserving the value of coordination and teamplay.

## Renowned Item

An item whose importance comes not just from stats, but from rarity, visibility, history, utility, or political significance.

## Hero Character

A socially important or long-lived character whose survival, capture, or death matters beyond ordinary combat outcome.

## Capture State

A defeat state where a character becomes a leverage object in the world without requiring the player to remain actively imprisoned in real time.

## World Memory

The accumulated social significance of characters, items, rescues, captures, and conflicts over time.

## Arena Mode

The detached lower-stakes combat lane that shares combat grammar with the world while remaining separate in progression and consequence.

## Project Spine

The central set of files and doctrines that define Neo UO’s identity, roadmap, and current direction.
