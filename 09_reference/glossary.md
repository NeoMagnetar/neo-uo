# Glossary

## Neo UO

The project name for this survival-war UO sandbox.

## Survival-War Sandbox

A world where survival, movement, tension, resource pressure, and social conflict matter as much as direct combat.

## Combat Grammar

The shared language of movement, timing, abilities, counters, and interaction that should remain coherent across both world and arena modes.

## Wound State

A lingering injury condition beyond simple current HP loss that affects readiness after severe combat.

## Capture State

A political or leverage-driven defeat outcome where a character is held as a meaningful world asset rather than instantly erased.

## Renowned Item

An item whose value comes not only from stats, but from rarity, social visibility, history, desirability, or political significance.

## Ambient Tension

Background world pressure created by risk, travel exposure, scarcity, and uncertain threat rather than constant combat spam.
