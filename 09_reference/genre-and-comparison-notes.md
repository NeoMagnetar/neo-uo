# Genre And Comparison Notes

## Purpose

Help frame Neo UO in a way that is understandable to both UO veterans and players coming from other survival or sandbox experiences.

This file is not meant to reduce Neo UO to another game.
It is meant to clarify the emotional and structural category the project sits in.

## Core Framing

Neo UO is best understood as:

- a UO-derived survival-war sandbox
- a high-consequence open-world conflict game
- a project where movement, scarcity, and pursuit matter as much as direct combat
- a world where conflict often produces aftermath rather than immediate clean reset

## What Neo UO Is Not

Neo UO is not:

- a standard shard with a few difficulty knobs
- a pure PvE progression game
- a nostalgia-only project
- a disposable arena-only game
- a direct clone of a modern survival title

## Useful Comparison Directions

Neo UO may resonate with players who understand:

- survival-world tension
- meaningful travel
- extraction and carrying pressure
- world-based social consequence
- political or guild-level conflict
- incomplete recovery after danger
- hunting and evasion rather than only equalized duel loops

## UO-Specific Lineage

Neo UO still uses the UO chassis and benefits from:

- recognizable world interaction
- skill-driven character thinking
- open-world conflict traditions
- item and loot significance
- sandbox identity

The goal is not to abandon UO lineage.
The goal is to use it as a strong foundation for a different world model.

## Survival-Game Emotional Lineage

Neo UO shares emotional overlap with survival games in that:

- the world itself matters
- movement matters
- injury matters
- carrying and extraction matter
- tension can exist before direct combat begins
- social encounters can have lasting consequence

## Important Comparison Caution

Neo UO should borrow useful language from survival and sandbox traditions without pretending to be a direct clone of those games.

The strongest framing is:
UO as the chassis for a different kind of world.
