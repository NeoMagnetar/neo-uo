# Genre And Comparison Notes

## Product Framing

Neo UO may be best understood as:

- a UO-derived survival sandbox
- an open-world warfare sandbox
- a high-consequence world with hunt-driven conflict
- a project with emotional overlap to survival games more than standard shard loops

## Useful Comparison Direction

The project can be discussed in terms familiar to players who understand:

- survival tension
- meaningful travel
- scarcity and risk
- political or social consequence
- lived-in open-world danger

## Caution

Neo UO should borrow emotional language from survival and extraction-adjacent design without becoming a copy of those games.
